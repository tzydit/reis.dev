# Surface brief: reis.dev home

Scope: the single-page home (`index.html`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: small-business owners and clinics looking for a freelance developer, often on a phone. Action: open WhatsApp with a project request. Proof: FinançasIQ and Dr. André Reis live, Money Tracker code, the fixed process. Constraint: static HTML, no invented claims; durations only from the FAQ (página institucional 1–2 semanas, sistema com login 4–8 semanas).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The visitor plans their own project before talking to Miguel; the page refuses the category's "hero + services grid + contact form" and the reference site's chat-and-approval demo.

OWN-WORLD: Dithered pixel sky (Bayer 4x4, eight blues) fixed behind the page; plastic white cards with layered inset shadows and one tight corner; Funnel Display light headings, Onest text; schedules drawn as dithered pixel bars; one pixel portrait; no mascots.

STORY: See a plan for your own project in seconds, see real products already shipped, understand the week-by-week process, send the plan on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: two-line headline at ~76px, one sentence, white primary button and glass secondary. Right: "Monte seu projeto" card with type and deadline chips, a dithered week-by-week schedule, what you receive, and "Enviar este plano no WhatsApp"; a small overlapping card shows FinançasIQ live.

FORM: Pinned by the user (agentes.chat style adapted). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
