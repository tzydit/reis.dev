# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of small businesses, clinics and independent professionals in Brazil who need a website or a custom web system and are looking for a freelance developer. They usually arrive from a referral, Instagram or a Google search, often on a phone, and want to know quickly whether this person can build what they need, how the work goes and how to start.

Secondary (not prioritized): recruiters and companies evaluating Miguel for a developer role.

## Product Purpose

reisdev.dev is the studio site of ReisDev Studio and the portfolio of Miguel Castanho Reis, a full stack developer in Rio Grande do Sul. It exists to turn a visiting prospect into a conversation. Success is a visitor opening WhatsApp to ask for a project.

## Positioning

A developer who ships and maintains real products of his own (FinançasIQ, a paid SaaS) and delivers client work end to end: scope and price fixed before code, a navigable version every Friday, deploy on the client's own accounts, code transferred to the client, 30 days of warranty.

## Operating Context

Remote work for all of Brazil; in-person meetings possible in Rio Grande do Sul. Contact by WhatsApp (+55 99 9429756) and e-mail (miguelcastanhoreisr@gmail.com). Response within 24 hours. Proposals follow a first conversation; fixed price for closed scope, hourly for open scope or maintenance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in `index.html`, no build step, deployed as a static site. External scripts only from public CDNs.
- Content language: Brazilian Portuguese.
- Stack Miguel works with: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, PostgreSQL, Python, Firebase, Docker, Vercel.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: ReisDev Studio. Visual signature: "reisdev studio" in lowercase, with "studio" at 75% opacity. Domain: reisdev.dev. Instagram: @reisdev.studio. Person: Miguel Castanho Reis.
- reis.dev is a parked domain we do not own; never link to it.
- Promise: "Prazo fechado antes do código." Concept: pixel sky, clarity and predictability.
- Symbol: the 8×8 pixel "r" (white cells at x2 y2–6, x3 y3, x4–5 y2, x6 y3) on the dithered sky tile. Always integer scales of 8.
- Palette: sky #2F66DB, sky dark #1D3F9E, sky light #5B8FF0, ink #151A24, white #FFFFFF, success #178A4A, alert #C93A2E. The eight SKY tones (#1F4FC4 to #BCD3F8) are only for the Bayer 4×4 dither.
- Type: Funnel Display 300 for titles; Onest for text; Doto 900 for numbers, weeks and labels (lighter Doto weights are unreadable at small sizes).
- Voice: first person singular, client language, short sentences. Show deliveries; never invent numbers, team or market leadership. Example schedules are labelled as illustrative.
- Brand kit: `branding/kit.html` generates Instagram posts, carousels, reel covers, story, avatar, highlight covers and the site icon from the same code as the site; `branding/export` holds the PNGs (`bash branding/exportar.sh` regenerates them). The `branding/` folder is local only and git-ignored, so it is never deployed with the site.
- The user likes the visual language of agentes.chat (dithered pixel sky, plastic-looking white cards, an interactive demo in the first screen) and wants similar ideas adapted to a portfolio, not a copy. Pixel-art mascots are not wanted.
