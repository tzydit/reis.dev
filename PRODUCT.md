## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of small businesses, clinics and independent professionals in Brazil who need a website or a custom web system and are looking for a freelance developer. They usually arrive from a referral, Instagram or a Google search, often on a phone, and want to know quickly whether this person can build what they need, how the work goes and how to start.

Secondary (not prioritized): recruiters and companies evaluating Miguel for a developer role.

## Product Purpose

reis.dev is the personal portfolio and studio site of Miguel Castanho Reis, a full stack developer in Rio Grande do Sul. It exists to turn a visiting prospect into a conversation. Success is a visitor opening WhatsApp to ask for a project.

## Positioning

A developer who ships and maintains real products of his own (FinançasIQ, a paid SaaS) and delivers client work end to end: scope and price fixed before code, a navigable version every Friday, deploy on the client's own accounts, code transferred to the client, 30 days of warranty.

## Operating Context

Remote work for all of Brazil; in-person meetings possible in Rio Grande do Sul. Contact by WhatsApp (+55 99 9429756) and e-mail (miguelcastanhoreisr@gmail.com). Response within 24 hours. Proposals follow a first conversation; fixed price for closed scope, hourly for open scope or maintenance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in `index.html`, no build step, deployed as a static site. External scripts only from public CDNs.
- Content language: Brazilian Portuguese.
- Stack Miguel works with: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, PostgreSQL, Python, Firebase, Docker, Vercel.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: reis.dev; person: Miguel Castanho Reis.
- The user likes the visual language of agentes.chat (dithered pixel sky, plastic-looking white cards, an interactive demo in the first screen) and wants similar ideas adapted to a portfolio, not a copy. Pixel-art mascots are not wanted.
